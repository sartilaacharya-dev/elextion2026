Small action. Real impact.

Why does your vote matter?

Elections are a chance to take part in decisions made on your behalf. Every eligible voter can help shape the conversation.

🏘️

Local choices touch daily life

From schools and roads to public services, local decisions affect the places we live, learn, and work.

🗣️

Representation starts with you

Voting is one way to tell elected leaders which issues matter to you and your community.

🌱

Participation builds a stronger civic life

When people take part, they help bring more voices and perspectives into public decisions.

Four steps to ready

A little planning goes a long way.

Voting rules and options differ by state. Use official election resources to check the details for where you live.

01

Check your registration

Look up your status and find your state’s registration deadlines at Vote.gov ↗.

02

Find out what’s on your ballot

Visit your state or local election office ↗ for official ballot and candidate information.

03

Choose how and when to vote

Ask your election office about in-person, early, and mail voting options where you live.

04

Make your plan—and follow through

Note deadlines, where to go, and what you may need. Share the plan with someone you know.
